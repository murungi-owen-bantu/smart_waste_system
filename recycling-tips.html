Smart Recycling Practices

🗂️ Separate Waste at Sources

Sort plastics,paper,glass,and organic waste into separate bins before disposal.

🌿 Compost Organic Waste

Turn food scarps into compost to nourish your garden and reduce landfill waste.

🔁 Reuse Before Recycling

Find new users for old materials to minimize overall waste generation.

🚫 Avoid Single-Use Plastics

Use refillable bottles and cloth bags to reduce plastics pollution.

Common Recycling Mistakes

❌ Mixing Organic & Plastic Waste

Keep recyclable materials dry and separate from organic waste.

⚡ Improper E-waste Disposal

Drop off old electronics at approved e-waste collection centers.

🚯 Non-Recyclable Bags

Use Clear or labeled recycling bags to make sorting easier.
