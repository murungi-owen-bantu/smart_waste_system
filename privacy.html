Priavcy Policy

At SmartWaste, your privacy is important to us. We collect information only to improve waste management services and ensure smooth communication with our users.

Information We Collect

  • Personal details during registration (name,email,phone).
  • Payment and transaction details for waste collection services.
  • Sensor and GPS data for waste pickup tracking.

How We Use your Information

Your data helps us schedule pickups,process payments, and notify you about recycling updates and environment tips.

Data Security

We use encryption and secure servers to protect your data. Access is strictly limited to authorized staff only.
